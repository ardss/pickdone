# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

To foster an open and welcoming environment, everyone participating in this project is expected to treat others with respect, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

**Encouraged**: friendly and professional communication, constructive feedback, openness to differing viewpoints, owning your mistakes.

**Unacceptable**: personal attacks and insulting language, harassment, posting others' private information, or any conduct a professional setting would find unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately via [Issues](https://github.com/ardss/pickdone/issues). Once confirmed, maintainers may remove comments and ban participants.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1.
